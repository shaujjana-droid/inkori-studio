FAQ

Common Questions

What file formats will I receive?
+
All products are delivered as high resolution PDF and/or PNG files, ready to print at home or at any print shop. Some products also include JPG format for digital use on tablets.
How do I receive my files after purchase?
+
After your crypto payment is confirmed, you will receive an email with a secure download link. Files are available instantly — no waiting required!
Can I print these as many times as I want?
+
Yes! All Inkori Studio products are for personal use and can be printed as many times as you like for your own personal use.
Which cryptocurrencies do you accept?
+
We currently accept USDT, USDC, BTC and ETH. We chose stablecoins like USDT and USDC so there are no price fluctuation surprises during checkout!
Can I use these for commercial purposes?
+
Our standard license covers personal use only. If you need a commercial license, please contact us and we can discuss custom licensing options.
